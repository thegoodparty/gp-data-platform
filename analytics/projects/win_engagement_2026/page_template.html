Who we look at

Win users with a race in 2026 (and fall 2025 races for reference). Reached voters = sent outreach (text, call, door knocking, phone banking) in the election's year, by election day. Power users = the most active people who reached voters: about the top 15% by days of campaign work in Win, such as sending outreach, building a voter audience or drafting a message. Just viewing the dashboard or onboarding doesn't count. 103 of 580 in 2026 (18%, because people tied at the bar all count).
